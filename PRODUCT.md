# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Pet owners in Limassol who want clear, trustworthy veterinary care for companion animals. They may be checking services, contact details, opening hours, location, clinic credibility, or real clinic/pet moments before calling or visiting.

## Product Purpose

Healthy Pet Veterinary Clinic's website presents the clinic, Dr. Avgoustinos Theodorou, veterinary services, product availability, real gallery moments, and direct contact paths. Success means visitors quickly understand the clinic's care, trust the practice, and know how to call, message, or visit.

## Brand Personality

Modern, premium, professional.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-generated landing pages, fake stock-photo clinic websites, overdecorated SaaS templates, excessive glows, random gradients, fake testimonials, and content that invents medical claims or clinic stories.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the clinic's real identity: use real assets, real information, and simple human copy.
2. Keep trust visible: contact, location, services, and working hours should stay easy to find.
3. Let photography carry warmth: visual moments should feel real and calm, not decorative or staged.
4. Make motion quiet and useful: animation should guide attention without delaying content.
5. Keep the site maintainable: editable content belongs in the structured content layer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear contrast, semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly navigation, meaningful alt text, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-safe animation. The site should remain readable and usable on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
